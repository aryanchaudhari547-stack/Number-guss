Rules and tutorial

The basics

Black and White take turns placing a stone on an intersection, Black first. Stones never move. Connected stones of one color form a group. The empty points touching a group are its liberties.

Capturing

A group with no liberties is captured and removed. Captured stones are prisoners and count as points for the player who took them.

Suicide and ko

You may not play a stone that leaves its own group with no liberties, unless that move captures enemy stones. The ko rule stops you from repeating the position that existed before your opponent's last move, so after a ko capture you must play elsewhere first.

Passing and ending

You may pass instead of playing. When both players pass one after the other, play stops and scoring begins. During scoring you mark groups that cannot survive as dead. If you disagree, press Resume play.

Scoring (Japanese rules)

Score = territory + prisoners. Territory is the empty points (and the points of dead stones) surrounded by only one color. Prisoners are the stones you captured plus the dead stones you remove at the end. Points between both colors count for nobody. White also gets komi (6.5 by default) because Black moves first.

Computer levels

Easy plays simple legal moves and sometimes takes easy captures. Medium reads liberties, captures and groups in atari. Hard plays thousands of fast practice games to choose its moves and takes a few seconds to think, more on bigger boards.
