Rules and tutorial

Goal

Player 1 (blue, bottom) must reach any square on the top row. Player 2 (gold, top) must reach any square on the bottom row. The first pawn to arrive wins. Each player has 10 walls.

Your turn: move or wall

On your turn you do one thing: move your pawn one square up, down, left or right (never diagonally), or place one wall.

Jumping

If the other pawn is right next to you, you can jump straight over it. If a wall or the edge of the board is behind that pawn, you may step diagonally around it instead.

Walls

A wall is two squares long and sits between squares. Walls cannot overlap, cross each other or go off the board. A wall is only allowed if both pawns can still reach their goal side. Tap Place wall, tap the board to preview (green is allowed, red is not), use Rotate to switch between horizontal and vertical, then press Place wall.

Tips

Turn on Show shortest routes to see the quickest path for both pawns (blue dots for Player 1, gold dots for Player 2). Save walls for moments when they make your opponent's path much longer.

Computer levels

Easy just walks the shortest way. Medium also places walls to slow you down when you are ahead. Hard looks several moves ahead for both sides and plans its walls carefully.
