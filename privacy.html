Welcome to Number Guess! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and how it is protected when you use our game.

When you use Number Guess, we may collect:

Your information may be used to:

3. Firebase Services

Number Guess uses Google Firebase services for authentication and database functionality. Firebase may process information such as account details and technical information needed to provide its services.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy Information

4. Leaderboard Information

If you participate in the leaderboard, your chosen username and game score may be visible to other users.

Do not use sensitive personal information as your public username.

We use Firebase security features and database access rules to help protect account and game information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Firebase and its service providers to operate the game, or disclosed where legally required.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account and game information may be retained while your account is active or as needed to operate the service.

You can contact us to request account or data deletion. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

8. Children's Privacy

We aim to provide a safe gaming experience. We do not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us.
