📖 How to Play

1. Pick a difficulty. A secret number is chosen for you.

2. Enter your guess. The game tells you higher or lower.

3. Find the secret number to win.

4. Scoring: 1000 points for 1 guess, minus 100 for every extra guess (minimum 100). Then it is multiplied by your difficulty: Easy x0.5, Normal x1, Hard x2.

5. Hard mode gives you only 10 guesses. Run out and you lose.

⚡ Powers

Win a game in 1 guess to permanently unlock 🔮 Oracle Hint. Once per game, it shows a small range that contains the secret number. It costs 200 points and does not count as a guess.

The leaderboard shows each player once, with their best score.
