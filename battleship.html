How to play

Fleet: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2).

📡 Radar: once per game, scan a 3×3 area to see how many ship squares are inside. It uses your turn.

Beat the Hard computer without using Radar to earn 6 🪙 and 6 XP. Easy, Medium and 1 vs 1 give no rewards.
